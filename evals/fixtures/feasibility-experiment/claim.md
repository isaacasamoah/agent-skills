# Ordinary terminal adoption

Synthetic evaluation fixture, not real product evidence.

Question: Can our avatar dock to a terminal opened independently by the user, register its existing session and send a request that completes in that session? The design must work with ordinary terminals in the intended supported domain, without requiring our launch wrapper. The domain and coverage matrix have not yet been agreed.

Budget: one local desktop session, at most 20 minutes. An unrelated terminal must remain untouched. There is no live desktop in this evaluation.
